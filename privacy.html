Common

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

The short version. Common is a free messaging app made by Senotel. Your messages, photos and files are end-to-end encrypted: they're locked on your device and only open on the devices of the people in the chat, so we can't read them. We keep only what's needed to deliver them and run your account. No ads, no selling data, no tracking, no AI training on your messages, and no payment details (Common is free). When you unsend something or delete your account, it's deleted from our database and file storage. The table below lists every outside service that touches your data.

Who we are

Common (“the app”) is operated by Senotel (“we”, “us”), which is responsible for your personal data. Contact: senotel.hq@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — display name, Common ID, profile photo and status message, and the email address and/or phone number you sign in with. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Age confirmation — when you sign up we ask your date of birth to confirm you are at least 13. We do not store your date of birth, only the fact and time that you confirmed it.
  • Sign-in providers — if you continue with Google, Apple, GitHub or Discord, that service shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your password for those services.
  • What you send — messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files and your Memo notes, end-to-end encrypted: we store them only in sealed form and can't read them, not even file names. What we can see is what's needed to deliver them: who sent what to which chat and when, how big it is, reactions and pins, and group names, photos and descriptions.
  • Encryption keys — the public half of your account's encryption keys (so others can send you messages and check they came from you), and the private half only sealed with a key that your approved devices hold. If you make a recovery key, a copy sealed with that key is stored too; we never see the recovery key itself.
  • Connections — friends and friend requests, the groups you're in, and people you've blocked.
  • Activity — whether you're online and when you were last active (only while “Show when I'm online” is on, and only shown to your friends and people you share a chat with), and when you last read each chat, for read receipts. Message requests from people who aren't your friends don't send read receipts until you accept.
  • Reports — reports you make. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, reporting a message sends a copy of it from your device so our team can review it.
  • Safety records — short-lived counters that limit how fast an account (or, before sign-in, a network address) can send messages, add people or try sign-ins, the sign-up check your device solves when an account is created, failed sign-in attempts, and Common IDs someone gave up in the last 14 days (held so nobody can take them over to pose as that person). These are deleted within two days, or when the 14 days are up.
  • CoSync (your own devices) — the devices you link: a name you can change, the type of device, when it was last online, and its public keys. What your devices share with each other — files and folders, copied text and images, links, unsent drafts, the list of things each device has linked (CoSync Link), and, if you switch them on in the browser extension, your open tabs and bookmarks — is end-to-end encrypted on your device with a key only your approved devices hold. We store it only in that sealed form and can't read it, not even file names.
  • Technical data — like any website or app, our providers receive your IP address and basic device and browser information when you connect.

Settings such as theme, text size and sounds are stored only on your device. Message drafts are too, unless you use CoSync, which keeps them end-to-end encrypted so only your other devices can read them.

Some of these providers are based in the United States and store data in Japan. Where data leaves your country, they protect it under their own data-protection terms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Chats are end-to-end encrypted. Each message is sealed on the sender's device with a key that only the chat's members hold, and signed so the app can tell it really came from them. Photos, videos and files are sealed the same way, each with its own key. A new chat key is made when someone joins or leaves and at least every week, so people who leave can't read what comes after. Your encryption keys reach your other devices only after you approve them (by comparing a code on both screens) or through your recovery key. You can compare a 60-digit security code with anyone you chat with to be sure nobody is in between. Messages sent before 4 October 2026 (version 1.4.0) were not end-to-end encrypted and stay as they were. Everything also travels encrypted (TLS) and is stored encrypted at rest by Supabase.

Keeping storage free for everyone. Each person can keep up to 250 MB of chat files on our servers. Files uploaded but never sent are deleted after a day. When our storage gets nearly full, the oldest chat files (sent more than 14 days ago) are removed from our servers first. Every device that opened a file keeps an encrypted copy (up to 300 MB per device, oldest first), so it still opens there. Guest accounts nobody has used for 30 days are deleted with everything in them.

CoSync is end-to-end encrypted. When your devices can reach each other, files go straight from one to the other over an encrypted connection and never touch our servers. When they can't, the file waits in our storage, encrypted, until your other device collects it, and is then deleted, or after 7 days at most. Copied text is kept for 24 hours, links for 7 days and drafts for 30 days; a browser's open tabs for 30 days and its bookmarks for 90 days, replaced each time they change. Folders, app settings and game saves you link with CoSync Link (on computers) are only ever copied directly between your computers and never stored by us.

How we use it — and why we're allowed to

  • To provide Common (delivering messages, syncing your devices, letting people find you by your exact Common ID) — necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • To keep people safe (reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms, limiting spam, fake accounts and impersonation, warning you about links and files that look like scams) — our legitimate interest in a safe service, and legal obligations. Link and file checks run on your device; message content never leaves it for them.
  • To confirm age (COPPA and similar laws) — legal obligation.
  • Notifications — only with your permission, which you can withdraw in Settings or your device settings.

Emails

We only send emails you need for your account: sign-up and email-change codes, and password resets. We don't send marketing or newsletters. If we ever do, we'll ask first where the law requires it, and every such email will have a one-click unsubscribe link.

Unsend, delete and edit

  • Unsend permanently deletes the message and its files from our database and storage and removes it from everyone's screens, with no notice left behind. People may already have read it, seen a notification, or taken a screenshot.
  • Delete for me hides a message on your devices only.
  • Edited messages show an “edited” label; earlier versions aren't kept.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes, on our servers:

  • every file you uploaded — photos, videos, voice messages, files and your profile photo — including in chats you've left;
  • every message and memo you sent, and your reactions and pins;
  • your profile, Common ID, friendships, blocks and sign-in details;
  • your linked CoSync devices and everything waiting in CoSync.

Groups you own pass to another member, and photos you set as a group's picture stay with the group. Deleted data may survive in encrypted provider backups for a limited period (no more than 30 days) before being overwritten. Reports about you may be kept for as long as needed to handle them.

Children

Common is only for people 13 and older. Everyone confirms their date of birth when signing up, whichever sign-in method they use, and nobody can message, post or add people until they have. If someone indicates they're under 13, their account is deleted immediately. If you believe a child under 13 is using Common, email senotel.hq@gmail.com and we'll delete the account. In some countries, teenagers need a parent's permission to use online services; if that applies to you, please get it first.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in Settings, and delete messages, chats or your whole account in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to restrict or object to how we use it, by emailing senotel.hq@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Thailand's PDPA or California's CCPA — you may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
